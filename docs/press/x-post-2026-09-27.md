# X post — 27 September 2026

Attach up to four of `x-shot-2026-09-27-*.png`. Each is 1200×1500 (4:5), the
tallest portrait X shows without cropping it in the timeline. `landing` first,
then `treasury` — the treasury is the thing nobody else has.

Every screen, in both widths, is in [`docs/media/tema/`](../media/tema/).

---

ViceHub was the only white thing on the screen.

It's tooling for GTA VI roleplay communities — the tab you keep open next to the game. The game is played after dark. The tool was a white page.

The logo's colours were already Vice City: magenta, violet, cyan, the orange of a sunset. They were just sitting on paper. So the page moved to where they belong.

That part is arithmetic, not taste. On white, the magenta scores 3.30 contrast and the cyan 1.81 — both fail as text, so every neon had to be a fill with dark type cut out of it. On #0B0711 the same magenta scores 6.04 and the cyan 11.04. A sign works the way a sign works: the colour lights up, the letters are the dark.

Then I opened all 30 screens in a browser and found four things that 2,580 tests had never seen. Three were older than the theme:

— Nobody had ever told the browser the page was dark. A checkbox in a crew's settings was a white square in a dark form. It had been a white square on a white form for months, which is exactly why nobody noticed.
— The stylesheet was painting checkboxes like text fields: full width, 48px tall, bordered. And the class whose whole job is to put the label beside the box never undid the column, so the box sat above the sentence it belonged to.
— The focus ring — the thing that says "your keyboard is here" — was moonlighting as a decorative halo around a green status dot.

None of them were broken code. They were the same rule written twice, and a browser is the only place that shows you.

The MVP is close. What is left is not code: a database, a host, a domain, and the payment keys.

ViceHub is tooling for GTA VI roleplay communities: run your crew, move what it earns, prove who actually turned up.
