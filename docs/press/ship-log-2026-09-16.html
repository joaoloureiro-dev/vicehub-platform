ViceHub Ship log · 16 Sep 2026

Applying was a place with no way back.

You asked to join a crew, and then nothing happened. The answer was already sitting on your page — accepted, or turned down, with the reason the leader wrote. Nobody ever went to fetch it. So people who got in never came in, and people who were turned down kept waiting for an answer that had already arrived.

That is the one I keep thinking about, and it is the smallest thing here. Eight changes shipped this week.

8 shipped 1,835 tests passing 0 skipped

What changed for the people using it

Signal

You are told when your application is answered

One nullable column, no new table. The application already knew when it was answered; what was missing was when you last looked.

Signal

An inbox for what actually needs you

Join requests, crews asking to play on your server, money waiting on a decision. Running three crews and a server meant checking four pages, over and over, forever.

Money

Every tier actually sells

Checkout took no plan at all and the webhook wrote the cheapest one for everything. A server buying the €99.99 tier would have paid €4.99 and received three crew seats.

Money

Plans named in the reader’s language

The single untranslated line on the pricing page was the one your eye lands on to choose what to pay for. The name now comes from the dictionary, like every other word on the page.

Yours

Delete your account

It refuses while something would be stranded by your leaving: money still in your wallet, a crew only you can run, a plan still billing a card. Each is something you could no longer reach.

Yours

Take your data with you

One file with everything we hold. No password hash and no session tokens — those are keys to the account, not facts about you, and a file in your Downloads is not where keys belong.

Access

Sign in with Google

Discord was generalised into a provider contract on the way, so the next one is a file rather than a rewrite.

Legal

Terms and a privacy policy written from the code

No analytics, no tracking, nothing sold. Two cookies, both strictly necessary — which is exactly why there is no cookie banner asking about them.
