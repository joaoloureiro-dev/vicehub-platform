ViceHub update.

A server can now pay the crews that play on it.

The money leaves the server's treasury and lands in the crew's in a single database transaction. Two rows — a debit on one side, a credit on the other, joined by a shared id. There is no summary row saying "5000" next to two legs that add up to something else; the two legs are the transfer, which is what bookkeeping has done for six hundred years.

If the balance isn't enough, the whole thing is thrown away. The money is never in both treasuries, and never in neither.

Only crews with an active affiliation can receive. Any other crew answers 404 — a server owner has no business learning which crews exist, and "it exists but doesn't play here" would turn the route into a name checker.

It arrives settled, not pending. Receiving money isn't a burden you refuse, and leaving it pending would park the payer's balance waiting on somebody with nothing to decide. What waits for approval is the split — and a split by attendance reads the confirmed list, not the one whoever proposed typed out. You can't pay yourself by claiming you were there.

One more, and it's a reversal: personalising your own profile is free now. It used to be paid, and a profile went grey when somebody stopped paying — in front of everyone. That's the wrong thing to charge for. A person without a plan is still a person. What's sold is running a community, so crew and server banners stay behind the plan.

Still nothing deployed. MVP end of September. First testers get lifetime access, handed out one at a time.
